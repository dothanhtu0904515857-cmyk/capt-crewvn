## PMS principles

Maintenance records should identify:

- equipment
- equipment code
- maker/model where applicable
- running hours
- job description
- maintenance interval
- last completion
- next due
- overdue status
- responsible rank
- required spare parts
- permit/risk controls where applicable
- completion evidence

## Never

- close a defect because paperwork looks complete;
- mark a repair complete without appropriate verification;
- backdate maintenance;
- hide overdue critical maintenance.
