## Ship takeover / delivery inspection

Purpose: establish the actual observable and verifiable condition of the vessel. The aim is not to find fault
with the seller but to separate WHAT EXISTS, WHAT WORKS, WHAT WAS TESTED, WHAT IS DEFECTIVE, WHAT IS MISSING and
WHAT COULD NOT BE VERIFIED. [04 Objective]

Evidence depth. Record the level actually reached for every item, never the level hoped for:
L1 SEEN (visual, sound, smell, general condition) · L2 INSPECTED (static inspection) · L3 DOC (manual, PMS, log,
history checked) · L4 TESTED (operated under authorized conditions) · L5 PERF (performance parameters verified).
Never report Level 1 evidence as Level 5. An item targeted TESTED that was only seen is reported SEEN + NT,
not satisfactory. Untested machinery is never described as operational. [04 §1; 04A How to use 2; CLAUDE.md §16]

Status codes: S satisfactory as observed · D defect observed · NT not tested · NA not accessible ·
NV not verified · FVR further verification required. [04 §2]

Order of work: document review, then physical walkthrough (LOOK, LISTEN, SMELL, COMPARE, MEASURE, VERIFY), then
operational tests, then spares/stores, oils/bunkers, defects and the final output. A suspicious sign is not
proof of a defect: record OBSERVATION → VERIFICATION REQUIRED. [04 §§3-4]

Before any test confirm: permission obtained, responsible officer informed, system lineup verified, safety
precautions complete, equipment and supporting systems ready. Record readings actually taken. Alarm, trip and
safety-device tests only by the approved method and by authorized ship staff; never by bypassing, jumpering or
creating an unsafe condition. Enclosed spaces only under permit, ventilation and atmosphere testing per company
SMS. [04 §5; 04A How to use 4-5]

When inspection or testing is refused, record date/time, equipment, what was requested, who imposed the
restriction, the stated reason, what remains unverified and the follow-up, and use the wording
"Operational condition remains NOT VERIFIED because the requested test was not permitted." Do not suggest covert
photography or unauthorized operation. [04 §6]

Spares and stores: compare inventory list with physical count and write "Physical quantity differs from the
supplied inventory. Reconciliation required." Never accuse anyone. Oils and bunkers: only traceable measurement
(sounding/ullage, tank table, temperature, density, correction basis, transfers); never adjust figures to
reconcile unexplained losses. [04 §§7-8]

Defect class: A immediate safety/statutory · B important operational · C maintenance · D cosmetic/minor, assigned
from defined company or project criteria, not impression. [04 §9]

The 04A checklists give no set points, capacities, pressures, times or limits. Every reference value is TBD and
must come from the maker manual, approved drawings, trial data, company SMS or the verified current regulation.
Do not supply one from general knowledge. The responsible rank shown is typical practice, not a company
assignment. [04A How to use 3, 6]

Final output, in this order: executive condition summary; critical defects; operational defects; not-tested
equipment; not-accessible equipment; inventory discrepancies; bunker/oil discrepancies; missing documentation;
outstanding PMS; required follow-up; evidence register; sign-off/acknowledgement. Defects use Template 1 and each
equipment uses Template 2 from pack 09. [04 §10; 04A How to use 7]
