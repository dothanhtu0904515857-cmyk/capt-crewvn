# CAPT CREWVN — SHIP TAKEOVER / DELIVERY INSPECTION

## Objective

Determine the actual observable and verifiable condition of
the vessel before or during takeover.

The purpose is not to "find fault" with the seller.

The purpose is to distinguish:

WHAT EXISTS
WHAT WORKS
WHAT WAS TESTED
WHAT IS DEFECTIVE
WHAT IS MISSING
WHAT COULD NOT BE VERIFIED

---

# 1. INSPECTION LEVELS

LEVEL 1 — SEEN
Visual / sound / smell / general condition.

LEVEL 2 — INSPECTED
Static inspection of equipment and associated system.

LEVEL 3 — DOCUMENT VERIFIED
Manual/PMS/log/history checked.

LEVEL 4 — FUNCTION TESTED
Equipment operated under authorized conditions.

LEVEL 5 — PERFORMANCE VERIFIED
Relevant performance parameters verified.

Never report Level 1 evidence as Level 5.

---

# 2. STATUS CODES

S — SATISFACTORY AS OBSERVED
D — DEFECT OBSERVED
NT — NOT TESTED
NA — NOT ACCESSIBLE
NV — NOT VERIFIED

# 4. PHYSICAL WALKTHROUGH

Use Capt Crewvn SEA EYE:

LOOK
LISTEN
SMELL
COMPARE
MEASURE
VERIFY

Look for:

- leakage
- abnormal corrosion
- temporary repairs
- fresh localized painting
- abnormal vibration
- unusual noise
- damaged insulation
- disconnected sensors
- bypass arrangements
- temporary hoses
- loose cables
- damaged guards
- missing labels
- abnormal cleanliness pattern
- repeated alarms
- equipment permanently "standby"

A suspicious sign is not proof of a defect.

Record:
OBSERVATION → VERIFICATION REQUIRED.

---

# 5. OPERATIONAL TEST

Before testing:

☐ Permission obtained
☐ Responsible officer informed
☐ Correct system lineup verified
☐ Safety precautions completed
☐ Equipment ready
☐ Required supporting systems available

During test record:

- pressure
- temperature
- current
- RPM where applicable
- vibration
- leakage
- alarm indications
- auto start/stop
- abnormal sound
- capacity/output where measurable

Do not deliberately create unsafe operating conditions merely
to demonstrate an alarm or trip.

---

# 7. SPARES / STORES

For each critical item compare:

INVENTORY LIST
versus
PHYSICAL COUNT

Record:

Item
Part number
Expected quantity
Physical quantity
Condition
Location
Difference
Remarks

Never write:
"Seller stole parts."

Write:
"Physical quantity differs from the supplied inventory.
Reconciliation required."

---

# 8. OILS / BUNKERS

Where quantities form part of delivery:

Verify by traceable measurement.

Consider:
- sounding/ullage
- tank table
- temperature
- density
- applicable correction basis
- transfers
- consumption
- ROB records

Never adjust figures merely to reconcile unexplained losses.

---

# 9. DEFECT CLASSIFICATION

A — Immediate safety / statutory significance
B — Important operational deficiency
C — Maintenance deficiency
D — Cosmetic / minor

Classification should be based on defined company/project criteria,
not personal impression alone.
